How MediShare Works in 3 Steps

A transparent and compliant redistribution pipeline ensuring unexpired medications safely reach those in need.

Step 1 • Donor Lists

💊 List Sealed Medicines

Donors enter medicine details, batch numbers, expiry dates, and upload a clear photo of the intact packaging.

Step 2 • Admin Verifies

🔬 Clinical Safety Review

Admins inspect packaging integrity, confirm the medicine has at least valid shelf-life, and approve it to the marketplace.

Step 3 • NGO Distributes

🏥 NGO Delivery to Patients

Authorized NGOs request needed medicines for free charitable care with real-time consignment tracking until delivery.
